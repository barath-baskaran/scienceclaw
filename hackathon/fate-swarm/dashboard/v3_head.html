What happens to this chemical in the environment?

Paste a structure (or an MS/MS spectrum) and get estimates of up to 12 environmental-fate properties (some are refused where the property does not exist for the compound) and 3 screening-level hazard calls. Each estimate comes with a likely range, how often that range held in testing, how new the chemical is to the model, and the EPI Suite estimate for comparison.
