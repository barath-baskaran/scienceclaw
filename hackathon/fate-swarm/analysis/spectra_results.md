All numbers below were printed from the files named next to them. Figures and tables are regenerated by `report_v31.py  [not redistributed; VM-internal]` from the same files.

- **Error scale:** test RMSE as % of the naive guess. The naive guess is the train mean **of the same compound subset**, so 100% means no skill.
- **Split:** labels_v2 cluster split (train/calib/test, never re-split). Every spectrum of a compound sits in one split, keyed by InChIKey skeleton. No AMOS spectrum is linked to more than one skeleton (checked: 0 of 268,542).
- **Skill bar** (PRINCIPAL, set before the runs): ≤85% of naive AND the upper bound of the 95% cluster-bootstrap CI < 100%.
- **Intervals:** split-conformal on calib, nominal 90%. Empirical coverage is always reported next to it.
- **Representation:** `REPRESENTATION_v31.md  [not redistributed; VM-internal]`, reviewed before any fit: W-PHYSICS GO, W-TOX GO.

## 2. P-002: OLD (Run 2) vs NEW representation, same compounds, same model. A clean negative.
Sources: `v31/eval_{pos,neg,ei}.json`; `v31/report/contrasts_v31.csv  [not redistributed; VM-internal]`; `v31/robust_control_v31.json  [not redistributed; VM-internal]`; `v31/report/old_vs_new_tally_v31.csv  [not redistributed; VM-internal]`; Figs. S-A, S-F.

- **Dimension-matched control:** NEW-DIM has 745 columns, OLD has 746.
  - With the main LightGBM, NEW-DIM beats OLD (paired CI excluding 0) in 0 of 10 ESI+ cells and 1 of 8 ESI− cells (WS +6.5).
  - The full-width NEW is significant only for WS (+3.7 ESI+, +5.6 ESI−).
- **Same comparison with three other learners** on the same compounds:
  - LightGBM with different hyperparameters: NEW better in 0/10 ESI+ and 1/8 ESI− cells.
  - Winsorised ridge: 2/10 and 0/8.
  - Cosine k-NN library search: 0/10 and 0/8. OLD is better in 1 ESI− cell (logKow, k-NN only).
  - Median Δ is +0.4 to +2.7 points (% of naive) across learners.
- **EI (nominal mass, quality-selected, with losses and differences)** beats Run 2's EI block in 3/10 cells with both LightGBMs (BP, MP, VP: +4.5 to +6.9) and 2/10 with ridge, but 0/10 with k-NN.
- **Finding:** rebuilding the representation did NOT rescue spectra for HR-ESI. The old representation was not the problem. This is a result in its own right, not a disappointment: the HR-ESI spectral signal is limited by what spectra carry and by the small label × spectrum intersection, not by featurisation.
- **EI is the exception.** The rebuilt EI block beats Run 2's on BP (n_test 216), MP (293) and VP (190) by +4.5 to +6.9 points. Caveat: EI libraries are a biased, GC-amenable (volatile, thermally stable) subset of the labelled chemicals, and the metadata-only control already reaches 76% on EI VP.
- **Verdict on P-002's claim** ("Run 2's representation, not the spectra, was what failed"): **false for HR-ESI, weakly true for EI.** Run 2's file already carried the measured precursor m/z, 1-Da neutral losses and mass-defect summaries. Keeping the fragment accurate mass and separating resolution classes buys almost nothing once that is in place.

## 3. CRITIC's challenges, answered
**(P-002.1) "The gain is the precursor mass."**
- With the shared fixed LightGBM, single-feature mass rungs overfit and score >100%. So the mass rung was refit with a regularised LightGBM and with a cubic ridge on log mass (`v31/mass_sensitivity_v31.json  [not redistributed; VM-internal]`).
- Regularised mass + adduct alone, ESI+: WS 77, VP 79, logKow 89, Koc 88. MP, Henry, BP, pKa ≈ 100.
- Fragments beyond that rung, paired CI excluding 0:
  - ESI+: logKow +15.3, MP +15.8, WS +7.3, Henry +7.4.
  - ESI−: logKow +10.6, MP +7.4, WS +9.5.
  - Not significant: VP and Koc.
- So the fragments do carry information beyond mass, but only on some endpoints.
- **Theoretical-precursor subset:** the mass rung is not better there (`by_theoretical_precursor` in eval json).
- **Jitter:** 3 or 10 ppm Gaussian jitter on test m/z changes any arm by ≤1.6 points.

**(P-002.2) "Pooling and popularity."**
- No feature arm contains counts or presence flags.
- **Metadata-only control** (counts per class, instrument fractions, number of CEs, number of sources, mean quality):
  - It is NOT empty. It has skill on EI VP (76%), and scores EI MP 83, EI Henry 84 and ESI+ VP 89. So library membership and acquisition metadata carry real information about volatility: which compounds get GC-EI vs LC-ESI spectra is itself chemistry-correlated (READER-1's subset-bias point).
  - The peaks beat metadata with CIs excluding 0 in 9/18 ESI cells and 7/10 EI cells (ESI+ Koc +38, logKow +28, WS +27; EI BP +25, WS +26) (contrasts META_ONLY→FULL / →EI).
  - No feature arm contains these columns.
- **One spectrum vs pooled top-5:** one spectrum is within ~5 points on all well-powered endpoints, and no CI excludes 0 apart from ESI− pKa-acid (n=34). So the pooled result is not a popularity artefact, and the dashboard ships the one-spectrum model.

**(P-005.3) "The domain flag in spectrum mode is circular."**
- The spectrum-derived flag is the max cosine of the query to train spectra (plus precursor distance in ppm).
- **HR-ESI: uninformative.** Spearman ρ with |error| is −0.11 to +0.22 with inconsistent sign. Coverage per cosine tercile is 0.75–1.0.
- **EI: weak but the right sign** (ρ −0.03 to −0.35).
- The dashboard therefore states that the ESI spectrum-only familiarity flag is uninformative.
- The structure nn_sim is also weak on these subsets (ρ −0.32 to +0.18).

**(P-005.2) Subset bias.**
- Every arm is scored on one compound list per endpoint×block (sha256 in eval json).
- Structure is reported both on the subset and trained on all compounds (STRUCT vs STRUCT_alltrain). The all-compound model is better in 8 of 28 cells with CIs excluding 0 (by 3.8–18.1 points: BP and MP in both ESI modes, and EI BCF/Henry/MP/WS), and never significantly worse. The spectrum-vs-structure gap quoted in §1 uses the all-compound structure model, which is the realistic comparator.
- Naive is the subset's own train mean.

## 4. Spectra + structure
- Adding the spectrum to known structure does not help: STRUCT→STRUCT+FULL Δ is −4.6 to +9.1 points, and within ±3 points on every endpoint with n_test ≥ 60.
  - Significant cells: ESI+ Henry −2.9 (worse), ESI− WS +3.0, ESI+ pKa-acid +9.1 (n=28).
  - EI: MP −1.5 (worse); the others are not significant.
- W-MODEL's independent pipeline (structure + physics + spectra) agrees: SPX−SP has a CI below 0 in 1 of 28 cells.

## 5. Limitations recorded
- **Theoretical precursors:** Czech Acad. Sci. (164,493 of 268,542 AMOS MS records, 61%) reports theoretical precursor m/z. The jitter arms show this does not matter.
- **Skeleton-level labels merge salts with parents.** Example: the pyridine skeleton has MP 141 °C, a salt value.
- **Low-n endpoints** (kOH, logKoa, pKa in ESI−, Koc in ESI−) are skipped or underpowered. n is stated in every table.
- **No low-resolution ESI model exists.** The predictor refuses nominal-mass ESI.
- An [M]+ ion cannot tell a permanent cation from a radical cation: 71 ESI+ library compounds with dominant [M]+ are mostly neutral aromatics. So for gas-phase/neutral endpoints the predictor warns rather than refuses (BB_EXPERT_NOTE_Q003).

## 6. Two-stage arm (spectrum → predicted substructure bits → property; MS2Tox idea, doi:10.1021/acs.est.2c02536 p.3): ATTEMPTED, INCOMPLETE
Scripts: `twostage_v31.py  [not redistributed; VM-internal]` → `_b` → `_fix` (3 attempts). Results: `v31/twostage_pos.json  [not redistributed; VM-internal]` (attempt 1), `v31/twostage_fix_pos.json  [not redistributed; VM-internal]` (attempt 3).
- Design: stage 1 was trained only on train-split spectra. Stage 2 used the same LightGBM class and parameters as the direct arm (PRINCIPAL's conditions).
- **Stage 1 never learned.** Its test macro-AUC over 381 substructure bits (trained on 21,225 spectra of 3,137 train compounds) was 0.528, 0.528 and 0.525 across three attempts. The fixes tried were (a) no subset-accuracy early stopping and (b) standardisation clipping. With stage 1 at chance, this is **not a test of the two-stage idea**. It is recorded as an unresolved implementation failure: the multilabel sklearn MLP on these features. The obvious next step, not done, is per-bit LightGBM, or SIRIUS/CSI:FingerID if it were installable.
- As measured (ESI+, same test compounds and spectra as the direct one-spectrum model):
  - Stage 2 trained on TRUE bits of all train compounds, applied to stage-1 probabilities (TS_true): much worse than the direct model on every endpoint (e.g. logKow 100 vs 76, VP 203 vs 75).
  - The same with probabilities thresholded at 0.5: also much worse.
  - Stage 2 trained on cross-fitted predicted probabilities (TS_cross): significantly worse on logKow (−7.3), WS (−5.6) and VP (−7.1); the other endpoints are not significant.
- By the pre-stated rule, the arm does not beat the direct model. Because stage 1 failed, the right conclusion is "attempted, incomplete", not "two-stage does not work".
